COUCH CUBE

Turn your Mac into a couch console.
Connect up to 4 controllers and play on the big screen in supported games.
Multiplayer support depends on the game, emulator and controller configuration.

1. Portable: extract the ZIP onto your gaming SSD and open CouchCube.app.
   On this Mac only: open the DMG and drag CouchCube.app to Applications.
2. Open the app from your chosen location. Applications installation is optional.
3. Choose On this Mac or On a portable SSD. New SSD? Prepare it at this first step.
   Already have files? Choose the existing drive without erasing.
4. Follow Storage, Game players, then Connect & play.
5. Your Home screen opens Steam Big Picture. Existing libraries return here.

Keep the app and your game library on the same SSD.
After moving the app within the SSD, use Settings > Update game library.
On a new Mac, check Game players for missing apps and configure controllers.

The manager and game data travel together; Steam, emulator cores and Wine
engines may still need host-side installation. No bootable operating system
is included. Apple Silicon, macOS 13+; individual games may need newer macOS.

Change storage revisits setup. Settings contains app updates and optional DMG backup.
Normal library creation never formats. A disk running this app cannot be erased.
SSD preparation downloads and verifies the latest public release before erasing,
then places CouchCube.app at the SSD root. No published release means no erase.
After erasure, a mount/copy failure cannot restore the original data.

App updates are checked on launch. Use Settings to view the latest release;
quit the app before replacing it. No automatic app replacement is performed.

This build is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Only open it if you trust the source.
